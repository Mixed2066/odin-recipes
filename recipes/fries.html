Description

Fries, also known as French fries, are one of the world's most popular side dishes and snack foods. Made from potatoes that are cut into strips, deep-fried until golden-brown and crispy on the outside, and left tender and fluffy on the inside, they are typically seasoned with salt and served hot. They are a staple of fast-food menus globally and are commonly enjoyed alongside burgers, sandwiches, or paired with favorite dipping sauces like ketchup, mayonnaise, and barbecue sauce.

Ingredients

  • Potato
  • Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Wash Potatos
  2. Cut Potatos
  3. Soak in Cold Water
  4. Dry and then cook in oil
